# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, public
or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
privately to the repository owner. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
